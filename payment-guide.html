Simple & Secure Payment Guide

Learn how to pay for your orders, make down payments, and reserve out-of-stock rice.

📱

GCash Full Payment

Instant

Best for items in stock. Your order is prepared and sent right away.

✔ Full Payment: Pay the total order amount and delivery fee through GCash.
✔ Quick Confirmation: Our team checks your payment screenshot within a few minutes.
✔ Fast Preparation: Your rice is packed and sent for delivery or pickup immediately.
💡 Please attach your GCash payment screenshot at checkout
💵

Cash on Delivery / Pickup

30% Deposit

Pay 30% first to reserve your rice, and pay the remaining 70% in cash upon arrival.

✔ 30% Down Payment: Pay 30% through GCash at checkout to hold your rice sacks.
✔ 70% Cash on Arrival: Pay the remaining 70% in cash directly to the rider or cashier.
✔ Guaranteed Rice: Ensures your sacks are saved and scheduled for delivery or pickup.
💡 You will receive an official receipt when you pay in cash
⏳

Pre-Order Reservation

₱0 Payment Now

For out-of-stock rice varieties waiting for new warehouse stock.

✔ ₱0 Upfront: No payment is needed right now while the item is out of stock.
✔ First Come, First Served: Your place in line is saved automatically in our system.
✔ 48 Hours to Pay: You only pay after new stock arrives and is assigned to you.
💡 Track your reservation spot anytime on your Profile page

How to Send Payment via GCash

Follow these 4 simple steps to complete your payment:

1

Scan QR or Copy Number

Open your GCash app. Scan the RiceFlow QR code or enter our official number: 0912 345 6789.

2

Enter the Due Amount

Enter the exact amount shown on your checkout screen (100% for full payment, or 30% for down payment).

3

Save Your Receipt

Save a screenshot of your GCash receipt showing the reference number, date, and amount paid.

4

Upload Screenshot

Attach your screenshot in the upload box at checkout, then submit your order. Our team will verify it quickly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about payments, orders, and reservations

What happens if my GCash payment screenshot is unclear or incorrect?

If your screenshot is blurry, missing the reference number, or shows the wrong amount, we will ask you to upload it again. You will receive a notification on your RiceFlow Profile page to upload a clear photo.

When do I pay for my out-of-stock rice reservation?

You do not need to pay when making a reservation. When new stock arrives and it is your turn, we will notify you through your RiceFlow Profile page. You will then have 48 hours to make your payment. If you do not pay within 48 hours, your reservation will be automatically cancelled.

Why is a 30% down payment required for Cash on Delivery or Pickup?

The 30% down payment guarantees that physical rice sacks are held exclusively for you. It prevents fake bookings and ensures our warehouse and delivery teams can schedule your rice accurately.
