How to Send Payment via GCash

Follow these 4 simple steps to complete payment verification

1

Scan QR or Copy Number

Open your GCash app and scan the RiceFlow QR code or enter our official mobile number: 0912 345 6789.

2

Enter Due Amount

Enter the exact amount indicated on the checkout summary (100% for full payment or 30% for down payment deposit).

3

Take a Screenshot

Save the GCash confirmation receipt screen showing the transaction reference number, date, and amount.

4

Upload & Submit

Attach the screenshot into the checkout upload box. Our admin staff validates the proof against our ledger.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding orders, payments, and cancellations

What happens if my GCash payment screenshot fails verification?

If the uploaded screenshot is blurry, missing a reference number, or contains an incorrect amount, our staff will mark the order as requiring resubmission. You will receive an SMS and in-app notification to upload a clear proof within your User Profile.

When do I pay for my out-of-stock Rice Reservation?

You do NOT need to pay anything upfront when creating a reservation. Once fresh harvest stock arrives at our warehouse and your position in the FIFO queue is reached, you will receive a notification giving you 48 hours to complete your payment.

Why is a 30% down-payment required for Cash on Delivery / Store Pickup?

The 30% down payment deposit guarantees that physical sacks are held exclusively for your delivery schedule, preventing fraudulent bookings and ensuring our local warehouse logistics run smoothly.
