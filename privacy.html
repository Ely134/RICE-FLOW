Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 17, 2026

Aurora Local Rice respects the privacy of customers and users of its online ordering platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected through the website, how the information is used, how it is protected, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

When using the website, we may collect information necessary to provide our services, including:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Contact number
  • Delivery address
  • Account information
  • Order and reservation details
  • Selected rice products and quantities
  • Payment information and uploaded payment proof
  • Order status and transaction records
  • Reservation queue information
  • Product reviews submitted by customers

2. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used to operate and provide the services of the website. This may include:

  • Creating and managing customer accounts
  • Processing customer orders
  • Processing product reservations
  • Managing reservation queues
  • Verifying submitted payment proof
  • Preparing orders for pickup or delivery
  • Providing delivery information
  • Verifying orders using Order QR Codes
  • Displaying order and reservation status
  • Maintaining transaction records
  • Responding to customer concerns
  • Improving the services and operation of the website

3. Payment Information

Customers may upload payment proof when making a GCash payment. Payment proof is used for manual verification of the customer's transaction.

The system does not directly process or connect to a GCash banking API or third-party payment gateway. Payment verification is performed by authorized personnel through the system.

4. Order and Reservation Information

Information related to orders and reservations is stored to allow the system to process transactions, manage inventory, maintain reservation queues, and provide customers with order status information.

Customers may see information associated with their own orders and reservations through their account.

5. Order QR Codes

The system generates Order QR Codes for order verification. The QR Code is associated with the corresponding order record and may be scanned by authorized personnel during pickup or delivery.

The camera used for QR scanning is only accessed when the QR scanning function is being used. The system does not intentionally store photos or videos captured by the device camera through the QR scanning feature.

6. Information Sharing

Customer information is intended to be accessed only by authorized personnel who require the information to perform their assigned responsibilities.

Personal information will not be intentionally sold or publicly disclosed for unrelated purposes. Information may be disclosed when necessary to process an order, fulfill a service, address a legitimate business concern, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

7. Data Security

Reasonable security measures are implemented to protect customer and transaction information from unauthorized access, alteration, misuse, or disclosure.

Access to administrative and staff functions is controlled according to assigned system roles. Sensitive information is restricted to authorized personnel based on their responsibilities.

8. Data Retention

Account, order, reservation, payment verification, and transaction information may be retained for legitimate business and system-management purposes, including maintaining transaction history, preparing reports, managing customer orders, and resolving transaction-related concerns.

Information may be removed or retained according to applicable business, legal, and system requirements.

9. Customer Responsibility

Customers are responsible for keeping their account credentials secure and should not share their passwords or other account information with unauthorized individuals.

Customers should also ensure that the personal information they provide is accurate and updated when necessary.

10. Privacy Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when changes are made to the website, system features, business processes, or applicable privacy requirements. The updated version will be posted on the website with its corresponding revision date.

11. Contact and Privacy Concerns

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding personal information collected through the website, users may contact Aurora Local Rice through the official contact information provided on the website.

By continuing to use the website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
